Privacy Policy

App Name: RCC Design
Developer: Kalai Constructions
Effective Date: October 29, 2026

1. Introduction

Thank you for using RCC Design. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use our Android application. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

RCC Design is a structural design calculator. We, the developer, do not collect, store, or receive any personal information from you. Specifically:

  • We do not ask for your name, email address, or any account or identifying information.
  • We do not collect your location.
  • We do not access your contacts, camera, microphone, or personal files.
  • We do not use our own analytics or tracking tools.

3. How the Calculations Work

All design calculations in RCC Design (beam, slab, column, shear, footing, pile cap and deflection design) are carried out entirely on your device. The values you enter and the results are not sent to us or to any server. If you use the "Download PDF Report" button, the report is saved only on your device, in your Downloads folder, or in the location you choose.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

RCC Design is free and shows advertisements provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and use certain information from your device, such as your advertising ID, IP address, device and operating system details, and information about how you interact with ads. We do not receive this information, and it is not linked by us to any design data you enter.

  • Learn how Google uses data from apps that use its services: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Google's advertising policies and privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy

5. Your Choices

  • Consent: If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or another region that requires it, the app shows a consent message the first time it starts. You can accept, decline, or change your choice later through that message.
  • Personalised ads: You can limit ad personalisation, or reset or delete your advertising ID, in your device's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads, depending on your device).

6. Internet Permission

The app requests internet access only so that advertisements can be loaded. The design calculations themselves work without an internet connection.

7. Children's Privacy

RCC Design is a professional engineering tool intended for adults and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect any data from children under 13 years of age.

8. Changes to This Policy

If we make any changes to this Privacy Policy, we will update the effective date above. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
